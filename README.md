### 🚀 Currently building: [Aceva](https://aceva.vercel.app)

**An AI interview coach for job seekers.** Paste a job description and your resume. Aceva predicts the company's interview loop, finds your skill gaps, interviews you by voice round by round, and scores you until you're interview-ready.

- 🎯 **Predicts every round** of the hiring loop, from the AI screen to the final panel
- 🎙️ **Voice mock interviews** with hands-free mode, speaking pace and filler-word feedback
- 📊 **Hiring-committee verdicts** on each answer, with specific fixes and a stronger rewrite of your own answer
- 📈 **Readiness score** that tracks your progress toward interview-ready

**[Try Aceva →](https://aceva.vercel.app)**
